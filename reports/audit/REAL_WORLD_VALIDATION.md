## 3. Scientific Root Cause: Procedural vs Genuine Radar Clutter

1. **Synthetic Noise Artifacts**:
   The demonstration SAR scenes included with the repository are procedural synthetics. Their background pixel distribution corresponds to independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) Gaussian white noise.
2. **Convolutional Filter Response**:
   Deep convolutional neural networks trained on genuine Sentinel-1 Sigma0 radar data learn spatial representations of multiplicative radar speckle (governed by Gamma and K-distributions) and ocean surface clutter (capillary waves, swell patterns, wind streaks). When presented with spatially uncorrelated white noise, the learned filters do not activate.
3. **Fail-Closed Safety Architecture**:
   Rather than producing false positive hallucinated segmentations, the ML model cleanly abstains (outputting sigmoids $<0.06$). The pipeline's architectural safety gating detects an empty ML mask on an oil sample and automatically routes to the classical dark-spot fallback detector, preserving end-to-end functionality while truthfully recording `detector_provenance = "CLASSICAL_DARK_SPOT_ML_FALLBACK"`.

On the 450 real Sentinel-1 SAR scenes of the held-out Part III evaluation split:

## 5. Attribution Language & Legal Honesty

- **Attribution Score Definition**: Vessel attribution scores generated by the pipeline are composite **heuristic likelihood indices** (0–100 scale), synthesized from:
  1. Spatial proximity to estimated slick origin.
  2. Temporal coincidence with slick formation.
  3. Trajectory consistency with drift vectors.
  4. Heading alignment.
  5. AIS continuity and speed anomalies.
- **Legal Non-Proof**: The score does **NOT** represent a calibrated posterior probability of guilt or legal culpability. Disclaimers are enforced across all UI screens, exported PDFs, Markdown summaries, and CSV reports.

## 6. Scientific Limitations & Incomplete Validation Notice

1. **Field Ground Truth**: Quantitative scientific validation against real-time maritime enforcement field investigations with independently certified slick sampling remains incomplete.
2. **Atmospheric & Oceanographic Lookalikes**: Low wind areas (<3 m/s), biogenic films, internal waves, and grease ice produce radar backscatter decreases nearly indistinguishable from mineral oil slicks under single-frequency SAR.
3. **Drift Advection Uncertainties**: Trajectory hindcasting relies on simplified wind drift factor (3%) and surface current advection. Hydrodynamic turbulence, Stokes drift, and weathering evaporation are modelled heuristically.
