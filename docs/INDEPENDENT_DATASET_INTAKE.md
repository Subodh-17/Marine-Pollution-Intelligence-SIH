## 1. Executive Summary & Rationale

During Phase 4.3A and 4.3B of the SIH-26143 project, the Zenodo Sentinel-1 SAR Oil Spill Dataset (`D:\zenodo_sar`, 3,020 paired scenes) was utilized for training (Part I & Part II), validation (Part II, 386 scenes), and initial release gating (Part III, 450 scenes). 

Because Part III was previously inspected and consulted to make candidate decisions, it cannot serve as an unbiased, independent validation set for final promotion of experimental multiscale models. An audit of local drives confirms that **no unconsulted, genuinely independent labeled SAR dataset currently exists locally**.

Consequently, the project release verdict is frozen at:
```
INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION = "BLOCKED_DATA_UNAVAILABLE"
RELEASE_VERDICT = "EXPERIMENTAL_CANDIDATE_FROZEN"
PRODUCTION_DEFAULT = "SINGLE_SCALE_BASELINE" (ML_MULTISCALE_SEGMENTATION=false)
```

This document establishes the mandatory intake protocol and evaluation freeze requirements that MUST be satisfied before any newly acquired dataset can be used to promote an experimental segmentation candidate to production default.

## 2. Dataset Intake Criteria

Any candidate independent dataset must undergo formal intake verification across seven dimensions before ingestion.

### 2.1 Provenance & Legal Clearance
1. **Source Verifiability:** The dataset must originate from a verified public repository, government agency (e.g., EMSA CleanSeaNet, NOAA, ESA, ISRO), or peer-reviewed academic publication. Direct URL, DOI, and publication citations must be archived in `docs/DATASET_PROVENANCE.json`.
2. **Licensing:** The dataset must carry an open, permissive license compatible with the SIH-26143 project (e.g., CC-BY 4.0, CC0, Open Government Licence, or explicit research/commercial permission). Proprietary datasets without license grants are strictly rejected.
3. **Data Protection & Ethics:** Imagery must be free of personal identifiable information (PII) or classified defense coordinates not cleared for public research.

### 2.2 Acquisition Conditions & Sensor Specifications
To ensure domain compatibility with the deployed `SpillUNet` and `SceneClassifier`:
1. **Sensor Family:** C-band or X-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR), preferably Sentinel-1 C-SAR (Interferometric Wide Swath - IW).
2. **Polarization:** Dual-polarization VV and VH channels. VV is mandatory for oil slick dark-formation contrast; VH is required for cross-polarization texture and vessel hard-target discrimination. Single-polarization (VV only) datasets require explicit zero-padding or model adaptation approval.
3. **Incidence Angles:** Scenes must be acquired within standard nominal incidence angles ($20^\circ \text{ to } 46^\circ$). Slicks in near-range steep incidence ($< 20^\circ$) or far-range shallow incidence ($> 48^\circ$) experience severe SNR degradation and must be flagged.
4. **Wind Speed Envelope:** SAR slick detection is physically valid between $2 \text{ m/s}$ and $12 \text{ m/s}$. If wind metadata is available, scenes outside this envelope must be segregated into stress-test subsets.

### 2.3 Image-Mask Pairing & Ground Truth Semantics
1. **Format:** Lossless GeoTIFF (`.tif`, `.tiff`) or 16-bit uncompressed raster. Lossy formats (`.jpg`, compressed `.png`) are disallowed for ground-truth masks.
2. **Resolution & Spatial Alignment:** SAR image channels (VV, VH) and corresponding label masks must share identical spatial dimensions, pixel spacing (nominal 10 m/pixel), and coordinate reference systems (CRS / WGS 84).
3. **Label Semantics:**
   - Ground truth masks must represent binary segmentation: `1 = Confirmed Oil Spill`, `0 = Sea Surface / Non-Oil`.
   - Multi-class datasets (distinguishing lookalikes, biogenic films, land, ships) must provide a documented mapping script converting labels to binary oil masks while archiving auxiliary classes for false-alarm auditing.
   - Ambiguous, unverified, or self-predicted masks (pseudo-labels generated by another neural network without human analyst verification) are strictly rejected as ground truth.

### 2.4 Preprocessing Compatibility
All images must be processed through the frozen production preprocessing pipeline:
- `sar_percentile_1_99_minmax_v1`: Robust 1st-to-99th percentile clipping followed by $[0, 1]$ min-max scaling per band.
- No dataset-specific manual contrast stretching, gamma adjustments, or ad-hoc filtering is permitted during evaluation.

### 2.5 Split Leakage Checks
Before any evaluation:
1. **Checksum Duplication Check:** Compute SHA-256 hashes of all raw image rasters. Any hash matching any image in `D:\zenodo_sar` (Parts I, II, or III) indicates direct duplication and causes immediate rejection.
2. **Spatio-Temporal Separation:**
   - Minimum geographic distance between intake scenes and existing training scenes: $\ge 50 \text{ km}$.
   - Minimum temporal separation: $\ge 14 \text{ days}$ for identical geographic track/frame corridors to prevent oceanographic slick persistence leakage.

## 3. Candidate Pre-Freeze Protocol

To prevent inadvertent test-set tuning and preserve scientific evaluation validity, the experimental candidate MUST be mathematically and programmatically frozen BEFORE any evaluation on the new dataset commences.

### 3.2 Immutability Requirement
Under no circumstances may any hyperparameter (seed threshold, min area, kernel size, coarse weight) be adjusted after observing performance on the independent dataset. Any hyperparameter change invalidates the test and requires acquiring a completely different independent dataset.

## 4. Evaluation Execution & Promotion Gate

When an eligible independent dataset is onboarded, evaluation must be executed via the automated release gate script:

```bash
python scripts/verify_release_gate.py --independent-dataset-dir <PATH_TO_DATASET> --independent-manifest <PATH_TO_MANIFEST>
```

### 4.1 Independent Promotion Gate Criteria
To promote `size_gated_closure` from experimental candidate to production default, the candidate must pass ALL of the following criteria against the single-scale baseline on the independent dataset:

1. **IND-01 (Zero Leakage):** 0 scenes overlap with Zenodo training, validation, or consulted test splits.
2. **IND-02 (Pixel-Pooled IoU Gain):** Overall pixel-pooled IoU must improve: $\Delta \text{IoU} > 0.0$.
3. **IND-03 (Large Slick Recovery):** Q4 large slick recall must improve by $\ge +2.0\%$ without loss of mean precision.
4. **IND-04 (Boundary Blooming Control):** Q1 small slick area ratio change must be $\le +0.02$ (no perimeter expansion).
5. **IND-05 (Lookalike Specificity):** False-positive pixel inflation on Lookalike scenes must not exceed $+5.0\%$ over baseline.
6. **IND-06 (Clean Sea Specificity):** Zero additional false-positive pixels on verified clean-sea (No-Oil) scenes ($0 \text{ px}$ delta).

If ANY criterion fails, the single-scale baseline (`ML_MULTISCALE_SEGMENTATION=false`) remains the production default.
