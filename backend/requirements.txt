# The trained SpillUNet/SceneClassifier checkpoints are served in production
# through ONNX Runtime's CPU execution provider (app/ml/onnx_engine.py), NOT
# through PyTorch. This is what makes genuine ML inference fit inside a

# unavailable and the classical dark-spot detector remains the operational
# default, exactly as it does without torch.
